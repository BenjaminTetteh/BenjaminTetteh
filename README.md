## 🚀 What I'm Building

### 🔐 ControlForge *(Flagship Project)*

ControlForge is an **Audit Operations Platform** built to modernize IT audit and governance.

Originally created to eliminate repetitive, spreadsheet-driven audit work, it has evolved into a security-first platform focused on:

- Identity & Access Governance
- IT Control Testing
- Evidence Onboarding & Normalization
- Findings & Remediation Workflows
- Executive Dashboards
- Audit Automation
- Governance Analytics

> **Community Edition** (Public) showcases the platform architecture and engineering practices.

> **Enterprise Edition** (Private) continues the commercial evolution of the platform with advanced governance capabilities and enterprise features.
